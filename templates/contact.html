Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is the theme detection?

Our theme detection algorithm has a 95%+ accuracy rate. We use advanced pattern recognition and maintain an extensive database of theme signatures to ensure reliable results.

Is the service free to use?

Yes! Our basic theme detection service is completely free. We believe in making e-commerce insights accessible to everyone.

Do you store the websites I analyze?

No, we respect privacy and don't store any personal data or website information from your searches. Each analysis is processed in real-time and not retained.

Can you detect custom themes?

Our tool is optimized for popular and commercial Shopify themes. While we can sometimes identify custom themes, the accuracy may vary depending on how heavily the theme has been customized.

How often is your theme database updated?

We update our theme database weekly to include new themes and improve detection patterns. This ensures we stay current with the latest Shopify theme releases.
