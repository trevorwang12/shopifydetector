Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is the theme detection?
Our theme detector has a 95%+ accuracy rate. We use advanced pattern recognition to analyze multiple aspects of Shopify stores including CSS files, JavaScript patterns, and asset structures.
Is the tool really free to use?
Yes! Our Shopify theme detector is completely free with no registration required, no hidden fees, and no usage limits.
Can you detect custom themes?
While we can detect many custom themes, our tool works best with popular Shopify themes. Custom or heavily modified themes may show as "Unknown" but we provide confidence scores to help you understand the reliability of results.
Do you store or track the URLs I check?
No, we don't store or track any URLs you submit. We're committed to privacy and don't collect any personal data or browsing information.
What if the tool doesn't work for a specific store?
If you encounter issues with a specific store, please contact us with the URL and we'll investigate. Some stores may have protection measures or unusual configurations that affect detection.
